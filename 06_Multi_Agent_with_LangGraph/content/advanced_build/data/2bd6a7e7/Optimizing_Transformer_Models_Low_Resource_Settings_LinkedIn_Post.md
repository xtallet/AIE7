### Key Highlights:

1. **LW-Transformer: A Lightweight Variant**  
   Imagine a Transformer model that works just as well but with a fraction of the computational load! Thanks to the work by Gen Luo et al., the LW-Transformer achieves this through a technique called Group-wise Transformation. It's proving to be a game changer for various tasks in low-resource applications.

2. **Transformers for Regression Mixture Models**  
   On another note, Reese Pathak et al. have demonstrated how flexible Transformers can learn regression models, even when data is scarce. They’ve shown impressive results with low mean-squared error, making it clear that these models are adaptable and efficient.
