**Understanding Student Loans for Low-Income Students**

1. **Types of Loans Available**:
   - **Subsidized Loans**: These loans are ideal for low-income students as the U.S. Department of Education pays the interest while you're in school at least half-time, during the 6-month grace period, and during deferment periods.
   - **Unsubsidized Loans**: These are available to all students, but they accrue interest while you’re in school.

2. **Income-Based Repayment Plans**:
   - For borrowers with federal student loans, Income-Based Repayment (IBR) plans are a valuable option. They cap monthly payments based on income and family size, which can significantly ease the financial burden for low-income borrowers. Payments may start as low as $0 if you meet certain income thresholds (source: [FinAid.org](https://finaid.org/loans/ibr/)).

3. **Federal Support Programs**:
   - Programs like the Income-Sensitive Repayment Plan allow payments to be adjusted based on annual income, offering further assistance to those with limited financial means.

4. **Important Considerations**:
   - As of September 1, 2023, the federal student loan interest rates resumed following the forbearance period during the COVID-19 pandemic, which may impact payment calculations. It's crucial to stay informed about any changes to federal policies or interest rates that might affect your loans.

Understanding your options and planning ahead is key to managing student loans effectively. We recommend that you explore the latest information on repayment plans and loans, as they can adapt to suit the unique financial situations of low-income students.
