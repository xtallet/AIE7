1. **Types of Loans**: Low-income students typically have access to both subsidized and unsubsidized federal student loans. Subsidized loans are particularly beneficial since the government pays the interest while the student is in school, allowing them to accrue considerably less debt overall. For a detailed overview of these loan types, you can visit [Federal Student Aid](https://studentaid.gov/understand-aid/types/loans/subsidized-unsubsidized).

2. **Income-Based Repayment Plans**: For those who may struggle with repayment after graduation, Income-Based Repayment (IBR) and Income-Sensitive Plans are available. These plans calculate monthly payments based on the borrower’s income, making it easier for low-income graduates to manage their loan repayments without falling into financial distress. More information regarding these plans can be found on [FinAid.org](https://finaid.org/loans/ibr/) and the [Federal Student Aid](https://studentaid.gov/manage-loans/repayment/plans/income-sensitive) website.

Payments have restarted in October 2023, and it is crucial for low-income borrowers to familiarize themselves with their repayment options to avoid potential defaults.
